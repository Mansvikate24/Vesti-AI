About Vesti-AI

Bridging computer vision and retail e-commerce to empower shoppers with digital confidence.

The Origin

Our Story

Vesti-AI was conceived as a fashion technology concept focused on making dress shopping more interactive through virtual try-on experiences. Online shopping has democratized access to thousands of designer collections, yet customers consistently face the frustration of not knowing how an outfit will look or drape relative to their own shoulders and frame.

Recognizing that physical fitting rooms cannot exist inside phone screens, we set out to investigate how modern web APIs—specifically in-browser machine learning and pose detection—could offer immediate, accessible visual previews without requiring specialized hardware, app installations, or cloud video harvesting.

🔭

Our Vision

To explore how artificial intelligence can make fashion discovery more engaging and accessible, helping online shoppers make thoughtful, informed decisions before making apparel purchases.

🎯

Our Mission

To connect fashion retail and emerging technology through practical, customer-centered digital experiences that respect user privacy, embrace honest transparency, and deliver joyful interactions.
