Vesti-AI Business Model & Economics

Explore our proposed commercial architecture, B2B SaaS tiers, direct e-commerce margins, and an interactive unit economics simulation designed for startup evaluation.

Section A

Business Overview & Target Segments

Vesti-AI is a fashion technology platform conceptualized to help fashion retailers and customers explore digital garment previews. Rather than building solely a standalone retail store, Vesti-AI combines an interactive customer portal with a dual-sided ecosystem supporting independent boutiques and fashion brands.

Potential Customer & Partner Groups:

1. Online Fashion Shoppers Individuals looking to visualize dress styles on their silhouette from the convenience of their laptop or smartphone.
2. Independent Dress Boutiques Local boutiques seeking affordable digital engagement tools and QR-enabled in-store mirror kiosks.
3. Emerging Fashion Brands Direct-to-consumer (D2C) apparel labels wanting to differentiate their online catalog experience.
4. E-commerce Marketplaces Web platforms interested in integrating client-side virtual try-on widgets into their existing checkout funnels.
5. Shopping Malls & Experiential Retail Physical retail centers seeking interactive touchscreens or smart-mirror QR installations.
Section B

Value Proposition

👗 For Customers

  • ✨ Interactive Dress Previews: Real-time alignment of transparent dress assets over live camera video or demo mannequins.
  • 🔍 Convenient Product Discovery: Filter dresses by color harmony, style tags, and season before purchasing.
  • 🎨 Personalized Shopping: Tailor Fit sliders allow manual scaling and fine-tuning to reflect personal comfort.
  • ⚖️ Easy Garment Comparison: Rapidly switch between silhouettes without waiting for physical dressing rooms.

🏪 For Retailers & Boutiques

  • 📱 QR-Linked In-Store Discovery: Physical hangtags enable shoppers to trigger virtual try-ons directly on their own phones.
  • 💡 Digital Fashion Modernization: Low-cost, web-based adoption without requiring specialized 3D scanner hardware.
  • 📈 Potential Engagement Improvements: Interactive visuals foster playful exploration and word-of-mouth social sharing.
  • 📊 Garment Analytics Framework: Track try-on session frequency and wishlist conversion to identify popular silhouettes.
⚠️
Evidence Transparency Note: While virtual try-on conceptually aims to improve buyer confidence, Vesti-AI makes no unverified empirical claims regarding proven sales lift or exact return reduction percentages at this pre-launch prototype stage.
Section C

Proposed Revenue Streams

Vesti-AI is designed with a hybrid B2C/B2B business model. All listed revenue channels represent proposed hypotheses for financial validation:

🛒

1. Direct Dress Sales

Retail gross margin (25%–35%) earned by selling curated dresses and gowns directly through the Vesti-AI e-commerce storefront.

🏢

2. Retailer Subscriptions

Recurring monthly SaaS subscriptions from participating fashion boutiques for catalog hosting, QR code generation, and studio integration.

💻

3. B2B Technology Licensing

Licensing the browser-based MediaPipe alignment engine and UI components as an embeddable SDK for third-party e-commerce stores.

📊

4. Premium Retailer Features

Advanced add-ons including custom branding, high-resolution garment asset processing, advanced traffic analytics, and priority server routing.

🏷️

5. White-Label Solutions

Tailored dressing room portals branded for larger department chains, regional retail groups, or mall kiosks.

Section F

Go-To-Market Roadmap

Structured in 5 progressive phases to validate customer appeal, refine computer vision accuracy, and evaluate boutique adoption.

P1

Phase 1: Prototype

Build & test functional browser prototype with MediaPipe Pose, Firebase database, and dress overlays.

P2

Phase 2: Boutique Pilots

Run closed pilots with 3–5 boutique partners using QR hangtags and tablet mirror displays.

P3

Phase 3: Feedback & Tuning

Collect user try-on feedback, test multi-angle lighting, and calibrate scaling accuracy heuristics.

P4

Phase 4: SaaS Introduction

Launch self-serve boutique onboarding portal with Starter (₹999) and Growth (₹2,999) plans.

P5

Phase 5: Brand Partnerships

Partner with larger regional e-commerce stores and evaluate 3D cloth deformation research.

Section G

Key Performance Indicators (KPI Framework)

The metrics Vesti-AI intends to measure systematically once commercially deployed:

Engagement
MAU & Session Count

Total monthly active users and number of virtual dressing room sessions launched.

Conversion
Try-On to Cart Rate

Percentage of users who add a dress to their shopping bag after a try-on session.

Checkout
Completion Rate

Proportion of initiated checkouts completed successfully through COD or UPI.

B2B Health
Retailer Retention & CAC

Monthly subscription churn rate and customer acquisition cost across boutique tiers.

Computer Vision
Landmark Tracking Rate

Fraction of camera frames with successful shoulder and hip pose identification without loss.

Post-Purchase
Estimated Return Rate

Returns tracking compared against industry baseline where reliable customer return data exists.
