💡 STYLING GUIDE

How to Identify Your Undertone

Use these four simple, time-tested fashion tests to find your undertone.

🩸

1. The Wrist Vein Test

Under natural daylight, look at your inner wrist. If veins look greenish or olive, you are warm. If they appear blue or purple, you are cool. If they look blue-green, you are neutral or olive.

👑

2. Gold vs. Silver Jewelry

Hold yellow gold and bright silver jewelry against your skin. If yellow gold illuminates you, your undertone is warm. If silver makes you pop, you're cool. If both look stunning, you're neutral. If antique bronze or rose gold looks best, you're olive.

☀️

3. Sunlight & Tanning Reaction

If you burn quickly and tan slowly, you lean cool. If you tan readily with golden or bronze depth, you lean warm. If you burn slightly then tan smoothly, you are neutral. If you develop a golden-ash olive tan, you're olive.

👗

4. White Fabric Test

Drape a sheet of stark, bright white fabric under your chin in natural daylight. Warm undertones look healthier with creamy off-white, while cool undertones look vibrant against pure, crisp white.
