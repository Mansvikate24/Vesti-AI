Stay Safe (सुरक्षित रहा)

5 Golden Rules every driver must know

🚨
NEVER enter UPI PIN to RECEIVE money!

पैसे घेण्यासाठी कधीही पिन टाकू नका. Money enters your bank directly. If someone asks for PIN to give you fare, IT IS A SCAM!

🔒
Never share your UPI PIN with anyone

Do not tell your PIN to passengers, friends, or even bank officers.

👁️
Always verify Receiver Name before paying

Always cross-check the shopkeeper or driver name displayed on screen before pressing Pay.

⚠️
Do not scan unknown or suspicious QR codes

Only scan QR codes pasted clearly at shops, petrol pumps, or auto stands.

🚫
Never share OTP or banking passwords

Bank OTP messages are private keys to your account. Never forward them.

Need Help? (मदत केंद्र)

Tap questions below for simple, step-by-step guidance

  1. Tap on Scan & Pay button.
  2. Point your phone camera steadily at the customer or shop QR code.
  3. Wait 1 second until the green frame beeps and detects the code.
  4. Check the receiver's name and amount on screen.
  5. Enter your 4-digit demo PIN and confirm!

Scan their QR code or enter their UPI ID. Enter the exact fare amount (e.g., ₹50). Confirm their verified name on screen, then type your Secret PIN to transfer.

A UPI ID is like a digital address for your bank account (example: sharmaauto@demo or 9876543210@bank). It allows you to receive money without revealing your bank account number or branch code.

It means money has successfully moved into the receiver's bank account. Both the sender and receiver will get an immediate confirmation message and sound notification.

A pending status means the bank servers or internet are taking extra time. Do NOT make another payment immediately. Wait 2–3 minutes and tap "Check Status" in your transaction history.

Payment failed means no money was deducted. Common causes include weak mobile network, incorrect UPI PIN, or bank server maintenance. You can safely try again.
